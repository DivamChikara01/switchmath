How SwitchMath works

  1. One switch. The space bar, Enter, the PRESS button, or any switch box that sends a key.
  2. Watch the yellow highlight. It moves through the choices on its own. Press when it is on what you want. (In step-scan: short press = next, hold = choose.)
  3. Go inside things. Choose a fraction to pick its top or bottom; choose a number or letter to replace it, add an exponent, or delete it. ↑ back goes up a level. undo, export and ≡ menu are in every list.
  4. The menu pauses scanning, starts a new equation, opens a saved one, changes speed. Your work saves itself on this device.
  5. Or talk. Turn on Voice in Setup (Chrome or Edge) and say things like “x squared plus 1 all over 2 equals y”, “denominator”, “select”, “undo”, “done”. The same commands can be typed.

Setup (for a helper)

Say or type math (“x squared plus one all over two”, “the quantity a plus b squared”) or commands (numerator, denominator, select, next, back, replace, delete, undo, export, done).

SwitchMath runs in this one file with no account. Equations are saved in this browser on this device. Nothing leaves the computer unless you turn on Voice, which uses the browser's speech recognition (in Chrome, audio is sent to Google). The switch is the Space bar, Enter, the PRESS button, or a switch interface configured to send Space or Enter.
